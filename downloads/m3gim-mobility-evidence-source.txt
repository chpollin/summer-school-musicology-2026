M³GIM mobility teaching sample: 40 statements, 23 documents, 8 places, 1952–1968.
Source: Digital Humanities Craft / M³GIM, Universitätsarchiv KUG. CC BY 4.0.
Selection: explicitly combined place-and-day statements from Box_1.csv, UAKUG/NIM_11 (object-table UAKUG/NIM_011), UAKUG/NIM_007 except press-review folio 17, and UAKUG/NIM_004 correspondence folios 22, 23, 27. Month/year-only dates are excluded; no days were invented. This is not a representative sample.
Each row is a recorded statement. Multiple statements from one document remain separate. Titles, roles, combined statements and cataloguing notes are preserved. Date and place are split from the explicit combined statement. context_note contains added teaching cautions. Sender, recipient and Malaniuk roles come from person links on the same folio. Missing values remain empty. No gender labels were inferred.
Guest-performance dates in receipts must be distinguished from payment dates. Document creation does not establish recipient location. Proposed events and uncertain dates do not establish attendance. Lowercase bayreuth is preserved; grouping may ignore case while retaining the source value.
source_url/source_csv_row identify the CSV record, counting the header as row 1, not the physical text line. object_source_url/object_source_row identify the object-table entry.
