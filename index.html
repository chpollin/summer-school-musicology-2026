Technical preparation
SESSION 1

Making Estate Materials Digitally Accessible

Follow an archival object through digitisation, catalogue description and digital access, using Stefan Zweig Digital. Examine how metadata and file relationships shape what researchers can find and use.

Slides, lecture notes & exercises →
SESSION 2

Large Language Models for Research Data Workflows: An Introduction

Explore how large language models work and how prompt and context engineering support research tasks. Connect model behaviour to transcription, metadata and the checking of generated results.

Slides, lecture notes & exercises →
SESSION 3

Large Language Models for Research Data Workflows: Hands-On Practice

Work with historical programme sources and examine the path from page images to transcriptions and structured statements. Retain evidence, distinguish roles and check the scope of each claim.

Slides, lecture notes & exercises →
SESSION 4

Promptotyping with Research Data

Use documented research requirements and reviewed data to develop a working prototype with an AI coding agent. Inspect the interface and its evidence links against concrete records.
